From file to measurement in four steps

  1. Open a file

    Photos, scans, screenshots or PDFs, one page or a whole document.

  2. Set the scale

    Draw over a scale bar, a printed dimension or a coin, and type its real length.

  3. Measure

    Results appear in real units and are listed as you go. Drag any point to adjust.

  4. Export

    Annotated image, PDF report, CSV, or DXF to continue in CAD.

The tools you need, nothing in the way

Every tool works on images and PDF pages, with a mouse, a trackpad or touch.

Scale calibration

Draw over any known length and type its value. Every result follows in real units.

Distance

Point to point, centre to edge, point to line and line to line.

Diameter and arcs

Centre and radius, three points on a circle, or arc length.

Angles

Three-point angles, or the angle between two separate lines.

Area

Straight or curved outlines, with the perimeter alongside.

Paths

Open or closed polylines for lengths that are not straight.

Point coordinates

X and Y positions from an origin you choose.

Perspective correction

Flatten a photographed surface from four corners before measuring.

Snapping

Snap to detected edges and to existing points for repeatable placement.

Questions

All questions

Are my files uploaded?

No. Files are opened and measured in your browser and never sent to a server.

How accurate is it?

As accurate as your reference and resolution. Use the longest known length you have and zoom in when placing points.

Can I measure PDFs?

Yes, single pages, page ranges or whole documents, with one scale applied across pages.

What does Pro add?

Unlimited measurements every day. Every tool and export is already included for free. See pricing.
